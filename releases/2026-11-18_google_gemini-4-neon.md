📘 Explained
What was introduced: Gemini 4 Neon

What problem it solves: High costs and slow execution speeds typically required by larger frontier models for complex, multi-step agentic workflows.
How it differs from before: It introduces four scalable thinking levels (low, medium, default high, and xhigh) to dynamically balance depth and efficiency at a lower cost per task.
Who benefits: Developers, enterprise teams, cybersecurity defenders, and consumers using the Gemini app or API.
Why creators and developers should care: It offers an efficient balance of depth and execution speed with adjustable thinking levels for building advanced agentic workflows.
