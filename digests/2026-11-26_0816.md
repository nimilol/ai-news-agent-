🔥 **OpenAI billing fix causes ChatGPT users to hit limits 40% faster** (📰 Report · P1 · GPT / OpenAI · 2 sources · 🕐 03:36 UTC)  
  OpenAI said a billing error had undercounted usage of its GPT‑6.2 Astra and Sol models. After the fix, users will reach their usage limits about 40 % faster on Astra and 25 % faster on Sol. The announcement was made by Thibault Sottiaux.  
  _Why it matters: Developers and power users relying heavily on GPT-6.2 models will experience a sharp decrease in available message allowances under their current subscription tiers._

⭐ **Z.ai launches GLM-5.4 for Coding Plan users without open weights** (📰 Report · P1 · GLM / Z.ai (Zhipu) · ⚠️ single source · 🕐 06:39 UTC)  
  Z.ai released GLM-5.4 to all GLM Coding Plan subscribers on November 23, offering 1.5 times the weekly quota of GLM-5.3. Unlike its predecessor, GLM-5.4 does not provide downloadable open weights or published API pricing, with developer access restricted to subscription tiers and security assessments.  
  _Why it matters: Users must choose between metered API access to open-weight models like GLM-5.3 or subscription-locked quota access to the newer Loop Transformer-based GLM-5.4._  
  [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/glm-5-4-vs-glm-5-3-no-open-weights-coding-plan-from-18-nov-26/)
