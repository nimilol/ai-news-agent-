## Claude / Anthropic

🔥 **Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2** (✅ Official · 17 sources · 🕐 09 Nov)  
  Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, both with a 1 million‑token context window. Claude Fable 5.2 is priced at $8 per M input tokens and $40 per M output tokens and is available to all users, while Claude Mythos 5.2 is limited to verified organizations. An optional fast mode for Fable 5.2 provides up to 2.5× higher output token throughput.
