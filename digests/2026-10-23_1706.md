## GPT / OpenAI

⭐ **OpenAI rolls out PIN-protected locked chats feature for ChatGPT** (📰 Report · ⚠️ single source · 🕐 15:23 UTC)  
  OpenAI has started rolling out locked chats to let users hide sensitive conversations behind a four-digit account PIN or device biometrics. Locked threads move to a dedicated section under Projects and disappear from general history and search to prevent screen-sharing leaks. Users who forget their PIN face permanent deletion of all locked chats and associated memories, as OpenAI support cannot recover them.  
  [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/23/chatgpt-locked-chats-pin-privacy-openai/)
