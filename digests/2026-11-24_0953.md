## 🚨 NEW MODEL RELEASES

🔥 **Z.ai unveils GLM-5.4 flagship model with loop transformer architecture** (📰 Report · P0 · GLM / Z.ai (Zhipu) · 9 sources · 🕐 23:40 UTC)  
  Chinese AI lab Z.ai has released GLM-5.4, its first flagship model built on a loop transformer architecture. Comprising 750 billion parameters, the model reuses a block of 28 middle layers multiple times per forward pass to achieve reasoning capabilities comparable to larger 2-3 trillion parameter models without a proportional increase in parameters. The model is currently accessible to Z.ai API Coding Plan subscribers.  
  _Why it matters: It demonstrates a major architectural shift toward layer reuse and iterative reasoning, offering high computational depth and performance at a fraction of the parameter scale._
