## Claude / Anthropic

🔥 **Anthropic introduces FlashProve for cryptographic AI model verification** (📰 Report · 2 sources · 🕐 22:41 UTC)  
  Anthropic is developing an open-source system called FlashProve to provide cryptographic proof verifying which specific AI model generated a given text output. The system uses published model fingerprints to protect against labs or third parties secretly swapping cheaper models or inflating performance claims.  
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-working-on-a-system-to-prove-ai-models-identity/)
