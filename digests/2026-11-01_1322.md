⭐ **OpenAI implements strict device and payment card limits on ChatGPT** (📰 Report · 2 sources · 🕐 12:05 UTC)  
  OpenAI is introducing new security measures that cap the number of paid ChatGPT accounts linked to a single device or payment card. Users hitting the limit will see a 'Device limit reached' error and can no longer use affected accounts on that device, a policy aimed at curbing the sharing of cheap flat-rate subscriptions.  
  [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-is-steering-chatgpt-users-toward-more-paid-usage/)
