## GPT / OpenAI

🔥 **OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet for AI agent purchases** (✅ Official · 11 sources · 🕐 15:00 UTC)  
  OpenAI has introduced ChatGPT Wallet, enabling its “dots” AI agents to make purchases on behalf of users using payment tokens from major card networks such as American Express, Mastercard and Visa. Each transaction requires user verification (e.g., Face ID or fingerprint). The feature is being rolled out in a limited launch to U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers.  
  [openai.com](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet) · [testingcatalog.com](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-agent-purchases/) · [the-decoder.com](https://the-decoder.com/openai-rebuilt-ai-shopping-from-scratch-with-chatgpt-wallet/)
